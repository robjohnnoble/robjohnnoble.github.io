## Short bio

After a first degree in mathematics, I earned my PhD in mathematical biology at the University of Oxford, investigating interrelationships between host population immunity and pathogen population genetics. For my first postdoctoral position, with Michael Hochberg at the Institut des Sciences de l’Evolution de Montpellier, I switched my primary focus from infectious disease to cancer. I then pursued concurrent postdocs in Niko Beerenwinkel’s systems biology group at ETH Zurich and Hanna Kokko’s theoretical biology group at the University of Zurich.

Since July 2020 I have been a (Senior) Lecturer in applied mathematics at City St George’s, University of London, where I lead a research group focussed on mathematical and computational modelling of cancer evolution and treatment. I have substantial experience in teaching mathematics at all university levels and have supervised three PhD students to completion as primary supervisor. I am an Associate Editor of the [Journal of Evolutionary Biology](https://academic.oup.com/jeb) and the elected Vice President of the [International Society for Evolution, Ecology and Cancer](https://www.evocancer.com).
